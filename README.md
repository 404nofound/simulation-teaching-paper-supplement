# Research materials for written-response analysis in transportation engineering education

Supplementary materials accompanying **Simulation-Supported Teaching for Scenario Analysis and Judgment: Equal Scores, Different Explanations in Transportation Engineering Education**.

Release: 31 October 2026. The instruments were administered in Chinese. The files here are English translations for reporting and review.

### Scoring and item identifiers

- Learning-experience responses are coded from 0 (strongly disagree) to 4 (strongly agree). Each dimension is the mean of five items: perceived learning support (PLS), cognitive engagement (CE), perceived realism and interactivity (PRI), and learning satisfaction and behavioral intention (LSBI).
- The knowledge tests differ in length, content coverage, and cognitive demands. Their maximum scores are 10 (pre-test) and 20 (post-test). The four written-response items are Pre-Q8 and Post-Q13–Post-Q15. Normalized scores are pre-test total / 10 and post-test total / 20; the normalized difference is (post-test total / 20) − (pre-test total / 10).
- Scenario identifiers S1–S3 refer to the three scenario contexts; Q1–Q4 identify questions within each. Each scenario is worth 10 points. S2-Q4 and S3-Q4 include the instruction “Briefly explain your choice,” corresponding to the Chinese original.
- Item scores follow the original scoring criteria. The response-feature codes describe the expressed content separately; they do not replace or alter the original scores.

In the paired tables and CSV, rows correspond to the first question in each pair and columns to the second. Explanatory links use 0/1/2, actor relations and concrete comparisons use 0/1, and explicit errors use 0/1/U (U: uncertain). Each applicable feature has 174 paired observations. A `not_applicable` row indicates that a concrete comparison was not requested in both questions; its blank count is not zero.

The CSV columns are `question_pair`, `feature`, `applicability`, `row_category`, `column_category`, and `count`. It contains aggregate cells, not individual students' answers or identities. The research team manually coded the responses and checked coding decisions against the original answers, retaining uncertain classifications and boundary flags. Boundary flags identify uncertain error judgments, unresolved cross-answer references, or ambiguous applications of the coding criteria. The 615 boundary-flagged response records (197 distinct item-text units) include all 88 uncertain-error records (18 units). The coding protocol documents the operational decisions and final review procedure.
